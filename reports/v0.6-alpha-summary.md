Full tables in `reports/orm-performance.md`, `reports/db-performance.md`,
`reports/migration-performance.md`. The pg-mock column is client framing
plus a socket round trip with no query execution: a floor, not a ceiling.

suites. ASan, UBSan and LSan clean on all eight C++ fixtures. Details in
`reports/orm-qa-report.md`.

## Known follow-up (not a blocker)

Wiring the manifest's `[database]` section to server startup (ambient
connection for served routes) is explicitly out of scope: the host opens the
database today (`db_open_sqlite`/`db_open_pgsql` set the ambient handle) and
`migrate`/`seed` open their own. Documented in `docs/ORM.md`.
